How the silence remover works

  1. Upload an MP3, MP4, or another supported audio or video file.
  2. Use the amplitude threshold to decide what counts as silence.
  3. Shrink or remove the highlighted silent regions.
  4. Export a cleaned MP3, a cleaned MP4, or timeline-ready ZIP segments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remove silence from audio without installing desktop software?

Yes. The web app handles common silence removal tasks directly in the browser.

Can I remove silence from video and keep an MP4 output?

Yes. Upload a video file, review the silent sections, and export a merged MP4 when you are satisfied.

What should I do if the tool marks too much audio as silence?

Lower the amplitude threshold or reduce the shrink amount until the highlighted regions match your intent.

Why provide ZIP segments as well as a merged export?

The ZIP option is useful when you want the kept sections in sequence for other editing workflows.

FAQ

How do I remove silence from an MP3?

Open the main tool, upload the file, adjust the threshold, preview the detected silence, and export MP3.

Can I preview the silence before exporting?

Yes. The waveform and highlighted regions help you review what will be removed before you export.

Is this only for podcasts?

No. It is also useful for lectures, audiobooks, meetings, and spoken-word recordings.
